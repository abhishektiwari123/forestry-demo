## Executive Summary

Rainbow Children's Medicare Limited, operating as Rainbow Children's Hospital and BirthRight by Rainbow, is India's leading multi-specialty pediatric and obstetrics/gynecology hospital chain. Founded in 1999 by Dr. Ramesh Kancharla in Hyderabad, the company went public in May 2022 and operates 19+ hospitals with 1,935+ beds across 8 cities in 6 states.

**Key Highlights:**
- **Annual Revenue (FY25):** ₹1,570 Cr (~$188M USD)
- **Market Cap:** ₹13,183 Cr (~$1.58B USD) as of January 2026
- **EBITDA Margin:** 29.4% (Q1 FY26) - Among highest in Indian hospital sector
- **Network:** 19 hospitals, 4 outpatient clinics, 1,935+ beds
- **Market Position:** Leader in organized mother & child care segment controlling ~30% with competitors
