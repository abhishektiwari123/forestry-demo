# Haunter Documentary Story Scripts

## Story Option 1: The Night Hunter

**Theme**: Survival
**Arc**: A Haunter emerges at dusk to hunt for sustenance in ancient temple ruins, showcasing its spectral abilities and predatory nature.

---

### Segment 1
**Scene Description**: Wide shot of ancient temple ruins at twilight. Crumbling stone pillars covered in vines, orange sky fading to deep blue. Purple mist begins seeping through cracks in the walls.

**Narration**: "In the forgotten places of our world... where light surrenders to shadow... an ancient presence begins to wake."

---

### Segment 2
**Scene Description**: Close-up of stone wall with cracks. Purple gaseous form slowly materializes through the gaps, forming into Haunter's distinctive floating hands first.

**Narration**: "The Haunter has no use for doors. Walls and barriers... are mere suggestions to a creature of pure spectral energy."

---

### Segment 3
**Scene Description**: Haunter's face emerges from the purple mist, eyes glowing red, menacing grin visible. Ancient temple interior in soft focus behind.

**Narration**: "For over a hundred million years... these phantom predators have haunted the darkness... unchanged, unmatched, unstoppable."

---

### Segment 4
**Scene Description**: Mid-shot of Haunter floating through temple corridor. Stone pillars pass by as it drifts forward. Subtle breathing motion in its gaseous body.

**Narration**: "As dusk falls completely... the Haunter begins its nightly patrol. Tonight, like every night... it must feed."

---

### Segment 5
**Scene Description**: Haunter pauses, eyes tracking left to right. Purple mist swirls more intensely around its form. Alert, hunting posture.

**Narration**: "The ghost's senses extend beyond mere sight. It detects the warmth... the life energy... of potential prey."

---

### Segment 6
**Scene Description**: POV shot through temple archway looking out at darkened forest. Small Rattata visible in clearing, foraging. Moonlight filtering through trees.

**Narration**: "A Rattata, foraging in the ruins... unaware that it's being watched. Unaware that the hunt... has already begun."

---

### Segment 7
**Scene Description**: Close-up of Haunter's eyes narrowing, grin widening. Purple aura intensifies. Hands position ready to strike.

**Narration**: "The Haunter feels no mercy... no hesitation. It is, quite simply... doing what nature designed it to do."

---

### Segment 8
**Scene Description**: Haunter phases through temple wall, body becoming translucent then invisible. Purple mist remains where it disappeared.

**Narration**: "With abilities that defy conventional physics... the Haunter passes through solid stone as easily as we walk through air."

---

### Segment 9
**Scene Description**: Haunter materializes behind the Rattata in the clearing. Ghost emerges from shadow, hands extended. Rattata still unaware.

**Narration**: "Positioning is everything. The spectral predator appears precisely where it needs to be... directly behind its unsuspecting target."

---

### Segment 10
**Scene Description**: Close-up of Rattata suddenly freezing, sensing danger. Ears perk up. Visible breath as temperature drops. Fear in its eyes.

**Narration**: "The prey senses something... a sudden cold. An instinctive dread. But by now... it's already far too late."

---

### Segment 11
**Scene Description**: Haunter's spectral hand makes contact with Rattata. Purple energy flows from prey to predator. Visible cold effect, frost forming.

**Narration**: "The Haunter's touch drains more than warmth. It feeds on the very essence... the life force itself."

---

### Segment 12
**Scene Description**: Rattata weakens, stumbles, then flees into the underbrush. Haunter doesn't pursue, satisfied. Purple glow brighter, energized.

**Narration**: "A measured feeding. The Haunter takes only what it needs... leaving the Rattata weakened but alive. There will be other nights."

---

### Segment 13
**Scene Description**: Haunter floats upward slowly, rising above the temple ruins. Moon visible in background, clouds drifting past.

**Narration**: "Satiated, the phantom rises. In this moment... suspended between earth and sky... it seems almost peaceful."

---

### Segment 14
**Scene Description**: Wide shot of Haunter silhouetted against full moon. Ancient temple ruins below, forest extending to horizon. Majestic composition.

**Narration**: "These ruins have stood for a thousand years. And for perhaps just as long... this Haunter has called them home."

---

### Segment 15
**Scene Description**: Haunter descends back into temple through open roof. Purple mist trails behind. Settling into familiar chamber.

**Narration**: "Territory, shelter, hunting ground... the abandoned temple provides everything this solitary creature requires."

---

### Segment 16
**Scene Description**: Interior of temple chamber. Haunter floats in center, eyes dimming slightly. Purple mist swirls peacefully around the space.

**Narration**: "The night is still young. But the Haunter enters a state of rest... conserving energy until the next hunt calls."

---

### Segment 17
**Scene Description**: Close-up of Haunter's face, eyes half-closed, expression relaxed. Gentle pulsing of purple energy. Tranquil mood.

**Narration**: "In the quiet darkness... the ghost becomes almost serene. No longer predator... simply an ancient spirit at peace."

---

### Segment 18
**Scene Description**: Wide shot pulling back from temple. Night sky, stars visible. Temple ruins peaceful. Purple glow faintly visible through cracks.

**Narration**: "And so the cycle continues... night after night, century after century. The Haunter endures... eternal guardian of the forgotten places."

## Production Notes

### Chosen Story: Option 1 - The Night Hunter

**Rationale**: This story offers the best balance of:
- Visual variety (temple, forest, aerial)
- Character development (predator to peaceful rest)
- Documentary pacing (action and contemplation)
- Achievable animation (subtle movements)

### Key Visual Requirements

**Character Assets Needed**:
1. Haunter full body (multiple angles)
2. Haunter close-up face (various expressions)
3. Haunter hands (attack position)
4. Haunter materializing (translucent)
5. Haunter with energy effects

**Environment Assets Needed**:
1. Temple ruins exterior (twilight)
2. Temple corridor interior
3. Stone walls with cracks
4. Forest clearing (night)
5. Temple chamber interior
6. Night sky with moon

**Props/Effects Needed**:
1. Purple spectral mist
2. Energy drain effects
3. Moonlight rays
4. Vines and vegetation
5. Rattata (prey)

### Motion Prompt Priorities

Segments emphasizing:
- **1-3**: Materialization (gaseous to solid)
- **4-5**: Floating movement
- **6**: Static scene (prey establishing)
- **7**: Subtle tension (eyes, posture)
- **8**: Phase-shift (translucent effect)
- **9**: Silent approach
- **10-11**: Predation moment
- **12-15**: Floating, rising, descending
- **16-17**: Gentle pulsing, resting
- **18**: Static wide shot

### Audio Considerations

**Narration Style**:
- Opening: Mysterious, building atmosphere
- Middle: Educational, observational
- Climax: Tense but not overly dramatic
- Ending: Contemplative, reflective

**Sound Effects Themes**:
- Temple ambiance (echoes, wind)
- Night sounds (crickets, distant owls)
- Spectral effects (ethereal tones)
- Energy sounds (whooshes, hums)
- Natural atmosphere (wind, leaves)
