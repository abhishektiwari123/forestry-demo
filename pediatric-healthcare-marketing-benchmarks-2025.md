## 1. PEDIATRIC PATIENT ACQUISITION: Parent Decision-Making Factors

### Primary Decision Factors

#### Timeliness (Highest Priority)
- **Same-day or next-day access** is the single most important attribute for parents
- Once parents decide to seek healthcare, timely access becomes critical
- Many parents seek healthcare for reassurance that illness isn't serious or urgent

**Source:** [PMC - Factors influencing parental preferences](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7366445/)

#### Location & Convenience
- **Proximity to ED** is a significant factor in parent decision-making
- Travel time and same-day appointments are critical considerations
- Parents seek to minimize travel time and stress when choosing providers
- **Financial aspects** scored highest at 55% in satisfaction studies
- **Accessibility and convenience** scored 40.5%

**Sources:**
- [PMC - Parental preferences systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7366445/)
- [PMC - Parental satisfaction assessment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10973693/)

#### Reputation & Rankings
- Significant portion of U.S. News ranking score comes from surveys of pediatric specialists
- 86 hospitals ranked as "Best" in at least one specialty (2025-2026)
- Rankings evaluate survival rates, patient safety, infection prevention, nurse staffing
- Family-centered care, long-term recovery support, and minimal school disruption are weighted factors
- Parents verify insurance network coverage before selecting hospitals

**Sources:**
- [U.S. News Best Children's Hospitals 2025-2026](https://health.usnews.com/health-news/best-childrens-hospitals/articles/best-childrens-hospitals-honor-roll-and-overview)
- [U.S. News Rankings Announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-news-announces-the-2025-2026-best-childrens-hospitals-302576461.html)

### Complex Care Considerations

#### Children with Medical Complexity (CMC)
- Parents of CMC interact with an average of **13 different physicians** representing **6 subspecialties**
- Parents make decisions based on mental models informed by:
  - Problem-solving activities
  - Seeking understanding
  - Obtaining tests and treatment
  - Ongoing caregiving demands

**Source:** [PMC - Decision-making for CMC parents](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8804956/)

#### Information Needs
- Parents need substantial information about:
  - Child's health condition
  - Disease progression
  - Healthcare system navigation
- Parents with more hospitalization experience show:
  - Better understanding of child's condition
  - More active involvement in care planning
  - Stronger engagement with healthcare system

**Source:** [PMC - Parental involvement in decision-making](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6279730/)

## 6. COST PER ACQUISITION (CPA) METRICS

### Understanding CPA in Healthcare

**Definition:** Cost per acquisition measures the cost of acquiring a customer/patient
- For hospitals: "Acquiring" typically means when patient schedules an appointment
- CPA is a leading indicator of long-term campaign success and marketing efficiency
- Guides ongoing campaign optimization to lower acquisition costs

**Source:** [WebMD Ignite CPA Healthcare Guide](https://webmdignite.com/blog/understanding-how-cpa-actually-works)

### How to Calculate CPA

#### Formula
```
CPA = Cost Per Lead (CPL) ÷ Lead-to-Patient Conversion Rate
```

#### Example Calculation
- **Marketing Investment:** $50,000
- **Leads Generated:** 500 (scheduled appointments/form fills)
- **CPL:** $100
- **Lead-to-Patient Conversion Rate:** 25%
- **CPA:** $100 ÷ 0.25 = **$400**

**Source:** [WebFX Patient Acquisition Cost Guide](https://www.webfx.com/industries/health/doctors-offices-medical-practices/patient-acquisition-cost/)

### Key CPA Benchmarks & Ratios

#### Sustainability Threshold
- **LTV must exceed CPA by at least 3x** for sustainable growth
- Patient Lifetime Value (LTV) must be greater than Cost Per Acquisition
- Optimal scenario: Low CPA with high LTV

**Sources:**
- [Mercury Healthcare CPA Guide](https://www.mercuryhealthcare.com/blog/understanding-how-cpa-actually-works)
- [PatientGain Calculate Patient Acquisition Cost](https://www.patientgain.com/calculate-patient-acquisition)

### Multi-Channel Optimization

**Critical Success Factor:** Comprehensive channel attention
- Must optimize ALL campaign elements and channels:
  - SEM (Search Engine Marketing)
  - Display advertising
  - Social media
  - Email marketing
- Focusing on only 1-2 channels limits CPA optimization
- Balanced attention across channels = lowest CPA + highest conversions

**Source:** [Becker's Hospital Review - CPA as Revenue-Driving Metric](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/finance/understanding-cost-per-acquisition-as-a-revenue-driving-marketing-metric-in-healthcare/)

## 12. PATIENT PORTAL ADOPTION FOR PEDIATRIC PATIENTS

### Pediatric Portal Adoption Rates

| Age Group | Activation Rate |
|-----------|----------------|
| **0-11 years** | 36% |
| **12-17 years** | 41% |
| **18-21 years** | 62% |
| **Overall Average** | 39% |

**Study Sample:** 52,713 unique patients

**Source:** [PMC - Long-term Patterns of Patient Portal Use for Pediatric Patients](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6220688/)

### Parent Usage Statistics

- **Less than 50%** of parents use patient portal for child's pediatric healthcare visits
- In July 2016 study:
  - Patient portal introduced to **9,000+ families** with children managing asthma
  - Only **237 families (2.59%)** actually adopted it

**Sources:**
- [Patient Engagement HIT - Parental Patient Portal Use Rate Lower than 50%](https://patientengagementhit.com/news/parental-patient-portal-use-rate-in-pediatrics-lower-than-50)
- [PMC - Disparities in Pediatric Patient Portal Activation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8480543/)

### Healthcare Organization Adoption

- **90%** of healthcare organizations offer patient portal access to patients
- Pandemic significantly increased adoption:
  - Virtual care surged to keep patients safe
  - Sudden shift increased portal adoption by both providers and patients

**Source:** [TechTarget - Patient Portal Adoption Tops 90%](https://www.techtarget.com/patientengagement/news/366585192/Patient-Portal-Adoption-Tops-90-But-Strong-Patient-Use-Is-Needed)

### Disparities & Challenges

#### Equity Issues
- Disparities observed in patient portal adoption/use in adults also present in pediatric populations
- Highlights need for strategies promoting equitable access to patient portals
- Low rates of portal adoption and sustained use across study practices

**Source:** [PMC - Disparities in Pediatric Patient Portal Activation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8480543/)

#### Barriers to Adoption
- Overall low adoption rates
- Poor sustained use after initial activation
- Achieving high-level portal adoption requires:
  - Improved care coordination
  - Redesigned workflows
  - Targeted outreach to families

**Source:** [Digital Healthcare Research - Pediatric Patient Engagement](https://digital.ahrq.gov/ahrq-funded-projects/pediatric-patient-engagement-criteria-meaningful-use-stage-3)

### Strategies to Improve Adoption

#### Clinician Communication (Highest Conversion)
"The strategy that has the highest conversion rate for families is when the clinicians talk to the family and say, 'this is the way we communicate effectively between visits. This is how you can ask me questions.'"

#### Provider Instructions Critical
"Instructions from the practice on how to optimize portal benefits appear to be the key to helping parents use it effectively and appropriately and increase families' satisfaction with the experience."

**Source:** [TechTarget - Balancing Patient Portal Privacy and Access](https://www.techtarget.com/patientengagement/feature/Balancing-Patient-Portal-Privacy-and-Access-for-Pediatric-Care)

### Adolescent Considerations

#### Transition of Portal Management
- Most pediatric providers begin shifting portal management to child at adolescence
- Guardian privileges retracted at this time
- Laws vary state by state
- Typical threshold: **Age 12 or 13**

**Source:** [TechTarget - Balancing Patient Portal Privacy and Access](https://www.techtarget.com/patientengagement/feature/Balancing-Patient-Portal-Privacy-and-Access-for-Pediatric-Care)

### Best Practices for Increasing Adoption

1. **Clinician endorsement** - Most effective strategy
2. **Clear instructions** on portal benefits
3. **Improved care coordination**
4. **Redesigned workflows** to integrate portal use
5. **Targeted outreach** to specific family populations

**Source:** [Magview - 5 Tips to Increase Portal Adoption Rate](https://magview.com/blog/5-tips-to-increase-the-adoption-rate-of-your-patient-portal/)
