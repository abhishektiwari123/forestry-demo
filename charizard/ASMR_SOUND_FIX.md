# ASMR Sound Fix - Remove Unwanted Speech

**Date**: 2026-01-10
**Issue**: AI generating human speech/narration in videos
**User Feedback**: "there is someone speaking in video not sure why it is coming, only sound should come in asmr style"

---

## PROBLEM IDENTIFIED

**Current sound descriptions include terms that AI interprets as speech**:
- "grunt of pain" → AI adds vocal grunting sounds
- "shout of pain" → AI adds shouting/speech
- "heavy breathing" → AI adds human breathing sounds
- "impact grunts" → AI adds vocal sounds
- "struggling grunts" → AI adds speech-like grunts
- "determined grunts" → AI adds human effort sounds

**Result**: Videos have unwanted human speech/narration mixed with sound effects

---

## SOLUTION: ASMR-ONLY SOUND DESCRIPTIONS

**ASMR = Pure environmental sounds + animal vocalizations ONLY**

### What TO Include (ASMR):
✅ Environmental sounds:
- Fire crackling, roaring, whooshing
- Thunder rumbling, lightning cracking
- Wind whistling, rushing, gusting
- Impact thuds, crashes, collisions
- Electricity crackling, zapping, arcing
- Wing beats, flapping sounds
- Debris scattering, rocks tumbling
- Water splashing (if applicable)

✅ Animal/Pokemon vocalizations:
- Dragon roars (deep, powerful)
- Dragon growls (threatening)
- Dragon hisses (pain/anger)
- Reptilian sounds
- Creature vocalizations (non-speech)

### What NOT to Include:
❌ NO human sounds:
- NO "grunts" (sounds like human effort)
- NO "shouts" (sounds like speech)
- NO "heavy breathing" (sounds like human breathing)
- NO "gasping" (sounds like human reaction)
- NO speech-like sounds

## PATTERN FOR PAIN/IMPACT SOUNDS

### Instead of Human Sounds:
❌ "grunt of pain" → ✅ "dragon hiss" or "sharp dragon cry"
❌ "shout of pain" → ✅ "agonized dragon roar"
❌ "impact grunt" → ✅ "impact collision thud"
❌ "struggling grunts" → ✅ "body tumbling" or "movement sounds"
❌ "heavy breathing" → ✅ "wind sounds" or remove entirely
❌ "gasping" → ✅ "sharp dragon hiss"

### Use Environmental Impact Sounds:
- "impact thud"
- "collision crash"
- "body hitting ground"
- "debris scattering"
- "rocks tumbling"

### Use Animal Vocalizations:
- "deep dragon roar" (power)
- "sharp dragon cry" (pain/alarm)
- "agonized dragon roar" (severe pain)
- "dragon hiss" (anger/pain)
- "dragon growl" (threat)
