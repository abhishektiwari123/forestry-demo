# Dragonite Counterattack Storyboard

## Sequence Overview
A 4-panel storyboard showing Dragonite's counterattack after taking damage from Charizard's flamethrower.

## Best Practices Applied
- Explicit forbidden elements in each panel
- Camera angles specified
- 95%+ character consistency required
- Continuity elements (burn marks) tracked
- Validation framework checklist

---

## Panel 1: Dragonite Recovery Stance

### Scene Description
Dragonite recovering from the flamethrower hit, showing burn marks, transitioning from pain to fierce determination. Charizard visible in background, still in attack position.

### Camera Angle
Medium shot, side-angle, focusing on Dragonite's upper body and face

### Authorized Elements ONLY
- Dragonite (with visible burn marks on chest/shoulder)
- Charizard (background, maintaining attack stance)
- Volcanic valley environment
- Smoke/heat distortion from previous attack

### FORBIDDEN Elements (Must NOT appear)
- NO shields or barriers
- NO defensive auras
- NO healing effects
- NO new Pokemon
- NO human trainers
- NO text or labels

### Character Expression
Dragonite: Pain transitioning to fierce anger, eyes narrowing, jaw clenching

### Motion Description
Dragonite STAYS in position, body tensing, wings beginning to spread, NOT retreating, NOT backing away

## Panel 2: Dragonite Charges Dragon Pulse

### Camera Angle

### Authorized Elements ONLY
- Dragonite (burn marks visible, energy charging)
- Dragon Pulse energy (orange-purple swirl at mouth)
- Charizard (background, starting to react)
- Volcanic environment

### FORBIDDEN Elements
- NO shields
- NO defensive barriers
- NO retreating movement
- NO flying away from battle
- NO healing of burn marks

### Character Expression

### Motion Description

### Camera Angle

### Authorized Elements ONLY

- Dragon Pulse beam (traveling toward Charizard)
- Charizard (being hit or bracing for impact)
- Energy explosion/impact effects
- Volcanic environment

### FORBIDDEN Elements
- NO shields on either Pokemon
- NO missing or dodging
- NO retreating
- NO defensive barriers
- NO new elements

### Character Expression

Charizard: Shock/pain from impact
