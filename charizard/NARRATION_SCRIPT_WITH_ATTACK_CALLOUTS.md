## NARRATION STYLE GUIDE

### Tone
- Epic but respectful
- Nature documentary (David Attenborough style)
- Emphasize the power and skill of both fighters
- Build tension, then resolve with mutual respect

### Key Phrases
- "Devastating [attack name]"
- "Both Pokemon" (emphasize interaction)
- "The [size/power] difference means nothing"
- "Neither backs down"
- "Respect has been earned"

### Attack Callouts
**Format**: "*Action verb* + **bold attack name** + *result*"

Examples:
- "Charizard strikes with **a devastating Flamethrower**!"
- "Dragonite counters with **a devastating electric punch** - Thunder Punch!"
- "**Both Pokemon unleash their dragon energy** - Dragon Rage!"

### Camera Notes in Narration
- Call attention to key visual elements
- "Notice the size difference"
- "Watch as the flames connect"
- "See the electricity crackling"
- "The impact creates a massive crater"
