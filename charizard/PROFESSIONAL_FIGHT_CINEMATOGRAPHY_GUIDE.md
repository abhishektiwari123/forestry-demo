## PROFESSIONAL CAMERA ANGLES

### 1. **Bird's Eye View** (Overhead Shot)
**When to Use**:
- Establishing battlefield layout
- Showing spatial relationship between both Pokemon
- Dramatic moments (Seismic Toss throw, crater impact)

**Effect**: Creates sense of scale, omniscient perspective, vulnerability

**Our Application**:
- Seg14 (Seismic Toss throw): Show both Pokemon from above - throwing and falling
- Seg15 (Victory descent): Aerial view showing crater below

**Example from Pokemon**: Often used when Pokemon are knocked down or when showing arena overview

---

### 2. **Worm's Eye View** (Ground-Up Shot)
**When to Use**:
- Emphasizing size and power
- Making Pokemon appear dominant/imposing
- Before major attacks

**Effect**: Subject appears powerful, intimidating, heroic

**Our Application**:
- Seg02 (Dragonite arrival): Looking up as larger Dragonite descends
- Seg04 (Battle stances): Low angle showing both Pokemon's imposing presence
- Seg13 (Seismic Toss ascent): Looking up as they spiral into sky

**Example from Pokemon**: Frequently used for legendary Pokemon or when emphasizing type advantage

---

### 3. **Dutch Angle/Tilt** (Canted Camera)
**When to Use**:
- Moments of chaos/disorientation
- After major impacts
- Building tension before attacks

**Effect**: Visual unease, disorientation, tension, instability

**Our Application**:
- Seg07 (Both reeling): Tilted angle as both Pokemon spin apart from impacts
- Seg11 (Speed dive): Slight tilt emphasizing extreme velocity
- Seg14 (Throw moment): Tilt during separation to add chaos

**Example from Pokemon**: Used during confusion status, disorienting attacks, or when battle turns

---

### 4. **Hip Level/Eye Level** (Neutral Height)
**When to Use**:
- Main action sequences
- Clear visibility of fight choreography
- Establishing normalcy before dramatic angles

**Effect**: Natural, intimate, puts viewer at Pokemon's perspective

**Our Application**:
- Seg03 (Face-off): Eye level between both Pokemon
- Seg05, 06 (Attack exchanges): Clear view of attacker and target
- Seg09 (Dragon Rage clash): Eye level to see energy collision clearly

**Example from Pokemon**: Most common angle for standard battle actions

---

### 5. **Over-the-Shoulder (OTS)**
**When to Use**:
- POV moments
- Showing what one Pokemon sees
- Creating connection with fighter

**Effect**: Immersive, builds empathy, shows target from attacker's view

**Our Application**:
- Seg05 (Flamethrower): From behind Charizard's shoulder seeing Dragonite ahead
- Seg06 (Thunder Punch): From behind Dragonite seeing Charizard as target
- Seg12 (Grab): From behind Charizard as claws reach for Dragonite's wings

---

### 6. **Low Angle (Below Eye Level)**
**When to Use**:
- Power shots
- Dominant moments
- Victory poses

**Effect**: Subject appears larger, more powerful, heroic

**Our Application**:
- Seg16 (Respect): Low angle on Dragonite rising from crater
- Seg17 (Valley recognition): Low angle on Charizards on peaks
- Seg18 (Sunset flight): Slightly low angle making silhouettes heroic

---

### 7. **High Angle (Above Eye Level)**
**When to Use**:
- Showing vulnerability
- Defeat moments
- Environmental context

**Effect**: Subject appears smaller, weaker, or provides context

**Our Application**:
- Seg08 (Recovery): Slightly high showing Charizard struggling
- Seg15 (Descent): High angle showing exhausted Charizard descending

## DYNAMIC CAMERA MOVEMENTS

### 1. **Tracking Shot** (Following Subject)
**When to Use**: Following Pokemon during movement

**Our Application**:
- Seg01 (Patrol): Track Charizard flying over valley
- Seg06 (Thunder Punch): Track Dragonite's barrel-roll
- Seg11 (Speed dive): Track Charizard accelerating downward
- Seg13 (Ascent): Track spinning motion upward

---

### 2. **Dolly Zoom/Push In** (Moving Closer)
**When to Use**: Building tension, emphasizing reaction

**Our Application**:
- Seg03 (Face-off): Slow push in on both Pokemon's intense stares
- Seg05, 06: Push in on impact points
- Seg12 (Grab): Push in on claws gripping wings

---

### 3. **Dolly Out/Pull Back** (Moving Away)
**When to Use**: Revealing scale, aftermath, establishing context

**Our Application**:
- Seg02 (Dragonite arrival): Pull back revealing storm clouds and valley
- Seg08 (Recovery): Pull back showing separation between Pokemon
- Seg09 (Dragon Rage): Pull WAY back to show massive explosion scale
- Seg17 (Valley recognition): Pull back revealing multiple Charizards

---

### 4. **Rotating/Circling Shot** (Orbiting Subject)
**When to Use**: Showcasing 360° action, grapples, intense moments

**Our Application**:
- Seg03 (Face-off): Circle both Pokemon during standoff
- Seg12 (Grab): Rotate around grapple showing all angles
- Seg13 (Ascent): Spiral with their spinning motion

---

### 5. **Whip Pan** (Fast Horizontal Movement)
**When to Use**: Following fast action, switching between subjects quickly

**Our Application**:
- Seg05: Pan from Charizard's mouth to Dragonite's impact point
- Seg07: Whip between both Pokemon as they reel apart
- Seg09: Quick pan between both Pokemon charging Dragon Rage

---

### 6. **Tilt Up/Down** (Vertical Camera Movement)
**When to Use**: Revealing height, showing scale vertically

**Our Application**:
- Seg02 (Dragonite arrival): Tilt up from valley to storm clouds
- Seg11 (Speed dive): Tilt down following dive
- Seg13 (Ascent): Tilt up as they rise into sky
- Seg16 (Respect): Tilt up as Dragonite rises from crater

## FIGHT CHOREOGRAPHY TECHNIQUES

### 1. **Impact Frames**
**Definition**: Freeze or near-freeze frame at moment of impact

**Application**:
- Seg05: Slight pause as Flamethrower connects
- Seg06: Brief freeze as Thunder Punch strikes
- Seg14: Freeze at release moment before fall

---

### 2. **Stacking** (Visual Alignment)
**Definition**: Arrange fighters so attacks appear to connect from camera angle

**Application**:
- Seg05: Align Charizard's mouth → flame stream → Dragonite's torso in straight line
- Seg06: Align Dragonite's fist → electricity → Charizard's shoulder
- Seg09: Align both beams meeting perfectly centered

---

### 3. **Reaction Shots**
**Definition**: Show both attacker AND target's reactions

**Application**:
- Seg05: Charizard's effort + Dragonite's bracing
- Seg06: Dragonite's power + Charizard's pain
- Seg07: BOTH showing impact effects simultaneously

---

### 4. **Wide-to-Close Coverage**
**Definition**: Start wide (context) then move close (details)

**Application**:
- Seg03: Start wide showing valley, push in to faces
- Seg09: Start close on charging, pull out for explosion
- Seg12: Start wide showing approach, push in on grip detail

## RULES FOR PROFESSIONAL FIGHT CINEMATOGRAPHY

### Rule 1: Vary Your Angles
**Never use same angle twice in a row**
- Seg05 (side angle LEFT→RIGHT)
- Seg06 (side angle RIGHT→LEFT) ✅ Reversed
- Seg07 (dutch tilt) ✅ Different

### Rule 2: Match Angle to Emotion
- **Power**: Low angle/Worm's Eye
- **Vulnerability**: High angle
- **Chaos**: Dutch tilt
- **Scale**: Bird's Eye
- **Intimacy**: Close eye level

### Rule 3: Wide for Context, Close for Impact
- Establish with wide shot (both Pokemon visible)
- Push in for impact moment (attack connection)
- Pull out for aftermath (showing results)

### Rule 4: Camera Moves with Energy
- **High energy attacks**: Fast camera movement (whip pans, rapid zooms)
- **Power attacks**: Slow dramatic movement (gradual push in)
- **Aftermath**: Stillness or slow pull back

### Rule 5: Always Show Both Fighters (When Possible)
**Our Key Principle**: This is a BATTLE - keep both Pokemon in frame!
- 16/18 segments feature both Pokemon
- Even solo shots reference the opponent

## COMPARISON: STANDARD vs PROFESSIONAL APPROACH

### Standard Approach (What We Want to Avoid):
- Static side view for all attacks
- Same camera height throughout
- No camera movement
- Only showing attacker

### Professional Approach (What We're Implementing):
- ✅ Varied angles (Dutch, Worm's Eye, Bird's Eye, Eye Level)
- ✅ Dynamic camera movement (dolly, tilt, pan, rotate)
- ✅ Both fighters visible (attack + reaction)
- ✅ Angles match emotional beats
