## 8. KEY INSIGHTS FOR ICICI LOMBARD D2C STRATEGY

### Opportunities
1. **Retail Health Gap:** Only 2.9% retail share vs 33% for Star Health
2. **Digital Growth:** 22%+ CAGR in digital channel
3. **Large TAM:** 4.67 Cr taxpayers earning >Rs 5L
4. **Mobile Dominance:** 56.7% of online insurance via mobile
5. **Efficiency Gains:** SAHI claims ratio 63.63% vs GI 82.52%

### Challenges
1. **Broker Dependency:** 51.9% of business from brokers
2. **SAHI Competition:** Specialized health insurers growing faster
3. **Star Health Dominance:** 33% retail share, 7L+ agents
4. **Trust Building:** Need 96%+ claim settlement ratio

### Recommended Actions
1. Launch mobile-first D2C health insurance app
2. Target 4.67 Cr income >5L taxpayers
3. Focus on retail health (currently only 2.9% share)
4. Reduce broker dependency from 51.9% to <40%
5. Match SAHI claim experience (87%+ cashless, 96%+ CSR)
