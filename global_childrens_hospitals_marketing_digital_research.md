# Global Children's Hospitals Marketing & Digital Strategy Research

**Research Date:** January 7, 2026
**Total Sources:** 20+ web searches conducted
**Scope:** 8 leading global children's hospitals across USA, UK/Europe, and Australia

---

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive research report analyzes the marketing budgets, digital transformation initiatives, patient acquisition strategies, and technology implementations of eight leading children's hospitals globally. The research covers website performance, mobile app adoption, patient experience scores, social media engagement, content marketing strategies, SEO performance, and patient portal adoption rates.

**Key Industry Findings:**
- Healthcare marketing budgets average $9.1M (2023), up from $7.6M in 2022
- Healthcare organizations projected to spend $12B+ on local advertising in 2024
- Patient portal adoption has grown from 25% (2014) to 65% (2024) nationally
- App-based portal access increased from 38% (2020) to 57% (2024)
- Epic MyChart dominates patient portal technology across leading institutions
- Top 25% of healthcare landing pages achieve 20.4% conversion rates
- Online appointment booking increased from 2.4% (2015) to 32% (2020)

## INDUSTRY BENCHMARKS & TRENDS

### Marketing Budget & Spending

**Healthcare Marketing Budgets (2023-2025):**
- **Average Healthcare Marketing Budget:** $9.1 million (2023), up from $7.6 million (2022)
- **Budget as % of Revenue:** Up to 7% of annual revenue (2025)
- **Local Advertising Spend:** $12+ billion projected for 2024
- **Projected Growth:** U.S. healthcare advertising to grow from $22.4B (2022) to $29.2B (2028)

**Budget Allocation (2024 Survey):**
- **Paid Digital Advertising:** 12.5% of marketing budgets
- **Social Media:** 11.5%
- **Paid Traditional Advertising:** 9.5%

**Monthly Digital Marketing Spend Ranges:**
- **Small Independent Practices:** $1,500 - $4,000/month
- **Mid-sized Groups & Multi-location:** $4,000 - $10,000+/month
- **Hospitals & Large Systems:** Significantly higher, structured by service line and market
- **44% of Healthcare Companies:** Spend $5,000-$10,000+/month on digital marketing

### Patient Portal Adoption

**National Growth Trends:**
- **2014:** 25% of individuals accessed online medical records
- **2024:** 65% of individuals accessed online medical records
- **Growth:** 160% increase over 10 years

**Access Method Shifts:**
- **App-Based Access:** 38% (2020) → 57% (2024) [+50%]
- **Web-Based Access:** 60% (2020) → 42% (2024) [-30%]
- **Caregiver/Proxy Access:** 24% (2020) → 51% (2024) [+113%]

**Platform Preference:**
- Patients overwhelmingly use EHR developer apps (especially Epic MyChart)
- Also use health care organization and insurer apps
